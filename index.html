waldgängerparallel civilization

At that time I lost myself amongpeople. Everything was crumbling mefrom the inside. Any contact was hard,every word pulled me under. I tried toescape. Sometimes I found only ways toget distracted, but the last time Ifound the surest way to hide. Theforest helps everyone. There the socialroles fall away, and you become who youare.

Waldgänger is German for “one who goes intothe forest”: Wald means forest, Gänger meansone who goes.

This exhibition is a sublimation of personalproblems, mine and those of the photographerKirill Dyagilev.We both wanted to run away,and all June we hiked into the forest and downto the coast. We climbed trees, sat on rocks,watched birds. We were truly free. Thatfeeling is what lifted us, and life somehowbecame easier.

Back in the city, we hid in the photo lab:developing, printing, cutting, choosingframes. And all of it to call on everyone elseto become Waldgänger.

waldgängerparallel civilization

A hoarse sound slips past the edgeof hearing. A whispering hum rollsin and dissolves into the oxygen’sexcess. On the Baltic shore, a morningoccurred. A testimony.

Deeper, into the forest, through sandyburdock and living walls. You leanon pine legs, press your cheeks to thescored bark of birches. If only youcould open your eyes a little wider,stay a few sunsets longer, I would showyou the things sunk in the unknown.Other orders of life creep along theshorelines, wander into the woodsand pitch their camps.

Don’t touch. Hey, don’t try to unravelit. Take it in, and know that there issomething beyond me, beyond you, beyondall the others. And that something hasstirred again.

A group project by the art collective "Syp"
for the "Baltic Odyssey" music festival, held
by the Baltic Sea.

Participants: Yana Glazkritski,
Darya Grishkevich, Diana Warridozart
and Maryana Molcha

a sonic labyrinth of human rage. fortystraining voices screamed at once, eachfrom its own source of sound, in thosedays at fabula.

i built the project’s visual identity on themetaphor of a sound wave. each wave is madefrom the title of the audio installation:the words out of rage pile onto one anotherchaotically, in places breaking apart,in places sticking together, turning intoa scream.

besides that, i designed the poster, bookletsand social media posts, and also took careof the organization.

Hi! I’m Maryana Molcha. I thinkin pictures and turn ideas into visualsystems.

I find inspiration everywhere,especially in stains and puddles:in whatever is accidental, uneven andalive. Then systems thinking kicks in.From a single metaphor I grow an entirelanguage, from a poster or a postcardto a magazine or a zine.

I’ve worked in visual communicationfor three years. I started withan internship at the design studio“volya”, where I learned the basics andwas handed clever books on the sly.Later I became art directorof paprika_magazine: I reworked itsvisual identity, then hired and traineda team of designers.

What I don’t know yet, I learn fast.Let’s make something people will wantto talk about.
